# Managing your website

A guide for the Acumen Gate Academy team. No technical knowledge needed.

Sign in at **yourdomain.com/admin** with the email and password you were given.

Everything on this page can be changed by you, whenever you like, without asking
a developer. Changes appear on the website straight away.

---

## Your daily job: leads

When you sign in you land on the **Dashboard**, which shows how many people are
waiting for a call.

There are **two separate lead lists**, on purpose:

| List | Who's in it |
|---|---|
| **General enquiries** | Anyone who filled in the "Enquire Now" form anywhere on the site |
| **Seminar leads** | Students who told us they attended one of your college seminars, and saw their college's offer |

Seminar leads are warmer — they have already met you.

On either list you can:

- **Call** — tap the phone number to dial straight from your phone
- **Mark contacted** — so nobody gets called twice
- **Add a note** — record what happened on the call
- **Filter** — "Still to call" is usually the list you want
- **Search** — by name, phone or college
- **Download as spreadsheet** — opens in Excel

**Paid enrollments** is a third, separate list. These students have already paid,
so they need onboarding, not a sales call. Anyone whose payment didn't go through
also shows here, marked *Needs follow-up*, so you can call and help them finish.

## The most common changes

### Change the banner at the top of the site

**Banner, hero & contact details** → *Announcement banner*.

Change the message, change what the link opens, or switch the whole strip off.
Use it for anything: a scholarship test, an admission deadline, a new batch.

### Change a batch date, fee or faculty

**Batches, dates & fees** → pick *Offline* or *Online* → **Edit** on a branch.

⚠️ The fee here is **what students are actually charged online**. Please
double-check it before saving.

Each branch can have its own demo video — paste the whole YouTube address and
we'll pick out what we need.

### Add this year's results

**Results page**.

1. **+ Add a year** — e.g. "GATE 2027". You can add years forever.
2. Click the year, then **+ Add a student** for each result.

Each year has its own **Show on the website** switch. Keep just the current year
showing day to day, then switch every year on when you want to show your full
track record at a seminar or to a parent.

### Post a recruitment update

**News & Updates** → *+ Add a post*.

A title, a one-line summary, the post itself, and publish. Keep it quick — a page
of stale news is worse than no news page, so it's better to post short and often.

Tip: when a big notification drops (ONGC, BHEL, CPCB), posting the same day is
what gets you found, because that's when students are searching.

### Upload result photos and newspaper clippings

**Result & press images**.

Mark each one as a *Student result* or a *Newspaper clipping*, and choose where
it shows — the scrolling strip on the homepage, "In the News" on the About page,
or both.

Please fill in the **photo description**. It's what Google reads, and what a
blind visitor's screen reader says.

### Change a college's seminar offer

**Seminar offers by college**.

Each college has its own offer. Whatever you write is exactly what a student from
that college sees after they submit the seminar form.

### Add a new option to a form dropdown

**Form dropdown choices**.

If you open a new centre or start tracking a new referral source, add it here and
it appears in the form immediately.

## Two things to be careful about

### The big number

The homepage says **"10,000+ GATE Success Stories and Counting."** That is the
only headline number used anywhere on the site, deliberately.

Please don't add a different total somewhere else. If one page says 10,000
success stories and another says a smaller number of students trained, both
Google and parents notice, and it costs you credibility.

### How the About page and FAQ are written

They're written as plain, checkable facts rather than marketing language. That's
deliberate: it's what lets ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and Perplexity quote you
accurately when someone asks "which GATE coaching in Vadodara should I join?"

If you edit them, keep that style. "Faculty from IITs, PSUs and government
service, coaching in Vadodara since 2014" works. "The best coaching with amazing
results" does not.

## If something looks wrong

- **A photo isn't showing** — open that section and check an image is uploaded.
  Empty slots show a grey box rather than a broken image.
- **A change didn't appear** — hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R
  on a Mac).
- **You can't sign in** — your developer can reset the password in Supabase.
- **Something you deleted is gone** — deletions can't be undone. Use the
  **Show on the website** switch instead of deleting when you only want to hide
  something for a while.
